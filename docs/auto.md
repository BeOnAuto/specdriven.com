# Auto

![Auto](/images/pages/auto.png)

## Put Your Specs on Auto

Auto is the productized workflow for Narrative-Driven Development.

It helps teams turn product intent into a reviewable narrative, refine that narrative with rules and examples, and carry the reviewed intent into AI-assisted implementation.

## Why Auto?

Prompt-to-app tools give you a quick snapshot. Auto is for teams that need the product story to survive beyond the first demo.

You start by clarifying what you want:

- the domain
- the narratives
- the scenes
- the moments
- the rules
- the examples
- the information the product shows

Then Auto gives the team a product surface for reviewing and evolving that intent.

## How It Works

**Tell the story. Review the narrative. Build with intent intact.**

1. **Define behavior** in [Narrative-Driven Development](/dialects/narrative-driven), the method language Auto productizes.
2. **Review the narrative** as goals, outcomes, moments, rules, examples, and should statements.
3. **Use Auto's workflow** to support AI-assisted implementation from reviewed intent.
4. **Keep the narrative alive** as the product changes.

This is not prompt-and-pray. It is a workflow that puts a durable product story between the prompt and the build.
