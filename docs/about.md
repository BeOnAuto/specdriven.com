# About SpecDriven

SpecDriven is my independent publication about the history, practice, tools, and open questions surrounding spec-driven development.

I use it to document the field, publish arguments, compare approaches, and preserve the lineage behind ideas that are becoming more important as software is increasingly built with AI agents. This is the work my career has been building toward, and I take responsibility for its point of view.

## About me

I am [Sam Hatoum](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samhatoum/), a product and software practitioner with 30+ years of delivery experience. My work has moved through testing, behavior-driven development, domain modeling, collaborative discovery, specification, and AI-assisted software delivery.

I began using the phrase “spec-driven development” in my consulting work in 2016 and purchased specdriven.com in 2020. The [origins](/origins) and [timeline](/timeline) pages separate my own history with the term from the much broader history of specification in software.

Today I work on [Auto](https://on.auto), the product modeling platform I founded for software built with agents. Auto makes product workflows, rules, screens, and decisions visible so people can review what is going to be built and check the result against what they approved.

## The relationship to Auto

SpecDriven and Auto have different jobs.

- **SpecDriven** is my field-level publication: history, principles, competing approaches, tools, criticism, and personal perspectives.
- **Auto** is the company and product I work on.

My experience building Auto inevitably informs some of what I write here. Where a page discusses Auto or an idea in which I have a direct interest, that relationship is disclosed. Auto is covered in the [tool landscape](/landscape/auto) as one participant in the field.

Auto does not publish or maintain SpecDriven. The editorial point of view and responsibility are mine.

## Editorial approach

The goal is not to make every approach look equivalent. It is to make the criteria, evidence, relationships, and disagreements visible enough for readers to judge the argument for themselves.

Corrections, contrary evidence, and pull requests are welcome. Editorial decisions remain mine so the publication has a clear point of view and a named person accountable for it. See [Contribute and connect](/community) for the practical details.
