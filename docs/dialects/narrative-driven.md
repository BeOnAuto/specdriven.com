# Narrative-Driven Development

![Narrative-Driven Development](/images/pages/dialects/narrative-driven.png)

> **Historical note:** Narrative-Driven Development (NDD) is a retired method name. It is not a current approach readers need to learn before using Auto or understanding spec-driven development.

Sam Hatoum developed NDD through client delivery work at Xolvio beginning around 2018. It organized product discussions using specialist terms for capabilities, goals, outcomes, and behaviors over time, drawing on BDD, Domain-Driven Design, EventStorming, story mapping, and collaborative modeling.

In 2026, Auto retired both the public method brand and its specialist vocabulary. The useful delivery practices continued inside Auto under ordinary product language: capabilities, goals, outcomes, behaviors, rules, examples, screens, and decisions.

This page remains as a historical reference. Auto maintains the current explanation of what changed.
