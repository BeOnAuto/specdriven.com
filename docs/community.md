# Contribute and Connect

SpecDriven is Sam Hatoum's independent publication. It has a clear author and point of view, but the underlying work gets stronger when readers challenge claims, correct errors, and contribute missing evidence.

## Suggest a Change {#suggest-a-change}

The field is bigger than one person's view. Corrections, contrary evidence, and missing work are welcome. Here are some useful contributions:

- **Fix something:** Flag a typo, broken link, unsupported claim, or unclear section.
- **Add a tool profile:** Building a spec-driven tool? Add it to the [landscape](/landscape/).
- **Add a person:** Know someone who should be on the [notable people](/people) page?
- **Add a timeline entry:** Something missing from the [history](/timeline)?
- **Add a resource:** Books, talks, articles for the [resources](/resources) page.
- **Challenge the evaluation framework:** Think a [dimension](/landscape/evaluation) is missing or mislabeled?

[Open an issue or pull request on GitHub →](https://github.com/BeOnAuto/specdriven.com)

## Suggest an Approach or Format

Are you building a structured specification approach or format? It may belong in the field guide.

Useful submissions explain:

- **What it helps people specify**
- **The form it takes** and how repeatable it is
- **How it guides implementation, review, or verification**
- **The primary evidence** readers can inspect

The taxonomy is editorial, not a membership badge. If you disagree with it, submit the argument and evidence rather than trying to make every approach look equivalent.
